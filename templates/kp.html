About Pratta
Pratta is an Italian-origin decorative coating brand present in 15+ countries — Russia, Thailand, Saudi Arabia, USA, Italy, Germany. All materials are produced with European-grade raw ingredients — authentic Italian lime — meeting the highest international quality standards.

Pratta Thailand is the official brand representative in Southeast Asia, offering supply, professional application, and certified master training through the Pratta Academy.
